Limitations
  • TrashNet has 6 classes (cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic, trash) with no dedicated organic or e-waste class -- cardboard is mapped to "paper" and "trash" is mapped to "other" for this app's dashboard categories.
  • ~2,500 images total is a small dataset by deep-learning standards; accuracy on real-world photos (different lighting, backgrounds, phone cameras) will likely be lower than the test-set number above.
  • The "trash" class is the smallest (137 images total) and is the hardest for the model to learn reliably.
  • The 60% confidence threshold before falling back to Rekognition is a simple heuristic, not a calibrated probability.
