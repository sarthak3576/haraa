Assumptions & formulas

These are commonly cited, rounded estimates, not a certified life-cycle assessment. Each kg recycled is assumed to avoid the following, by material:

  • CO2 avoided = kg × (1.5 plastic / 0.9 paper / 2.0 metal / 0.3 glass / 0.5 organic / 3.0 e-waste / 0.2 other)
  • Trees saved = kg of paper × 17/1000 (basis: ~17 trees per tonne of recycled paper)
  • Water saved (litres) = kg × (90 plastic / 60 paper / 180 metal / 20 glass / 5 organic / 200 e-waste / 10 other)
  • Energy saved (kWh) = kg × (2.0 plastic / 1.0 paper / 4.5 metal / 0.3 glass / 0.2 organic / 5.0 e-waste / 0.3 other)

These figures are a guide to help you see the scale of your effort, not a precise measurement.
